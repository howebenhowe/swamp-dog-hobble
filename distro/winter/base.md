**prep**
1. the night before, freeze as many plastic bottles/jugs of water as your freezer can hold for faster cooling after the cook
1. peel then cut potato and butternut squash to 1/2" cubes
1. slice yellow onions
1. pop lids of coconut milk can\(s\) and tomato can\(s\)
1. arrange all ingredients near the stove

**cook**
1. heat big pot empty for 5 minutes
1. add one cup oil to bottom of pan, yellow onions, stir periodically for 5 minutes
1. add spice mix and garlic/ginger, stir across bottom of pot, let it stick and get fragrant for ~30 sec, then stir and ref-latten, repeat a couple times and at first sign of smoke, deglaze coconut milk to a slurry
1. add rest of coconut milk, canned whole tomatoes, potatoes, and butternut squash and stir
1. simmer until biggest pieces begin to tender, \~15 minutes
1. add cabbage, peppers, simmer for 5 minutes, then cut heat and transfer to two or more hotel pans
1. distribute ice wands across the pans
1. arrange pans on cooling racks outside with fan blowing air across them to cool quickly
1. once cool, combine with pickled cabbage, cooked & chilled beans, and chicken in coconut milk
1. wash your hand and arm to the elbow, mix stew thoroughly by hand, then split across buckets using a small pot to scoop
1. refrigerate overnight, [follow packing instructions](./pack) for other items
