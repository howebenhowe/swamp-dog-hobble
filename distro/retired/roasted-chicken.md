1. mix all ingredients in large cambro
1. marinade in fridge 6 hours minimum or overnight
1. lay out chicken on sheet trays
1. roast for 45 minutes at 350°, less if small pieces, until internal temperature reaches 165°
1. cool one hour
1. remove skins (save them)
1. remove bone
1. rough chop meat and set aside
1. lay out chicken skins on sheet trays
1. roast in oven at 375° until crispy, \~15 minutes \(keep a close eye, don't let them burn\)
1. remove skins from trays, cool briefly, then chop and set aside until ready to be added to rice
