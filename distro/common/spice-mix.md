**method 1 \(small batch\)**

1. toast whole spices together in a pan until aromatic, \~4 minutes
1. grind whole spices in blender/spice grinder
1. combine all spices
1. portion into quart containers

**method 2 \(huge batch, \~5 gallon yield approach\)**

1. preheat oven to 350°
1. toast whole spices in hotel pans for 5-10 minutes, until fragrant
1. grind whole spices in batches
1. using a >10 gallon pot as a mixing vessel, thoroughly blend spices with a combination of whisking and folding/chopping with a pastry knife. Ensure there are no pockets of unmixed spices along the bottom.
1. transfer spice mix to 5 gallon bucket with a rubber seal. Never allow moisture into the bucket.
