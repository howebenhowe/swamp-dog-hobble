**prep**
1. the night before, freeze as many plastic bottles/jugs of water as your freezer can hold for faster cooling after the cook
1. peel then cut potato to 1/2" cubes
1. cut cauliflower to bite-size florets
1. slice yellow onions
1. pop lids of coconut milk can\(s\) and tomato can\(s\)
1. open packs of chicken into one cambro
1. arrange all ingredients near the stove

**cook**
1. heat big pot empty for 5 minutes
1. add one cup oil to bottom of pan, yellow onions, stir periodically for 5 minutes
1. add spice mix, stir across bottom of pot, let it stick and get fragrant for ~30 sec, then stir and ref-latten, repeat a couple times and at first sign of smoke, deglaze coconut milk to a slurry
1. add rest of coconut milk, canned whole tomatoes, stir, then add chicken
1. simmer, stirring occasionally, until chicken is done
1. remove chicken and set aside to cool \(use tongs/perforated pan or your preferred method to remove the chicken\)
1. add potatoes, stir, and simmer until biggest pieces barely tender
1. add cauliflower, simmer 5 more minutes
1. cut heat, transfer to two or more hotel pans
1. distribute ice wands across the pans
1. arrange pans on cooling racks on kitchen table with fan blowing air across them to cool quickly
1. while base cools, pick chicken meat from bones and rough chop
1. once cool, combine with fresh chopped veggies, pickled cabbage, cooked & chilled beans, salsa verde and chopped chicken in big potatoes
1. wash your hand and arm to the elbow, mix balela thoroughly by hand, then split across buckets using a small pot to scoop
1. refrigerate overnight
