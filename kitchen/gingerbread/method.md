1. butter 8" pan
1. set oven to 350°
1. whisk together flour, baking powder, baking soda, ginger, cinnamon, cloves, and salt
1. heat water to near boiling in pan, remove from heat, stir in cubed butter
1. combine butter/water with dry ingredients
1. add eggs one at a time
1. add molasses
1. pour into pan, bake 35-45 minutes until inserted toothpick/knife returns clean
